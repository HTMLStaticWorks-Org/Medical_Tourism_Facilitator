A Clear, Reassuring Step-by-Step Patient Journey

Discover how we coordinate every stage of your international healthcare journey—from your initial enquiry and secure document transfer to hospital evaluation, travel logistics, and post-return communication.

Step-by-Step Coordination Timeline

The 7 Stages of Your Coordinated Medical Travel Journey

At every stage, we clearly separate our non-clinical coordination assistance from the treating hospital's independent medical decision-making.

Step 01

1. Submit a Treatment Enquiry

Begin by submitting a general enquiry outlining the medical specialty of interest, your preferred destination countries, and your preferred communication channel. No sensitive clinical records are required at this initial contact stage.

Patient Action Submits basic contact preferences, treatment category of interest, and preferred travel timeframe.
Facilitator Coordination Role Assigns a dedicated International Patient Coordinator to review destination options and explain required medical documentation.
Hospital Clinical Role Provides general department availability guidelines and required diagnostic checklist for initial case review.
Step 02

2. Share Medical Information Through an Appropriate Secure Channel

Following your initial briefing, your coordinator provides a dedicated, encrypted hospital transfer portal link so you can securely transmit existing diagnostic scans (MRI, CT, X-ray), lab results, and medical summaries.

Patient Action Uploads existing diagnostic reports and imaging through the designated encrypted channel with informed consent.
Facilitator Coordination Role Verifies that files are legible and properly formatted (e.g., English translation where required) before routing to the hospital desk.
Hospital Clinical Role Receives medical records within its secure international patient system for specialist physician review.
Step 03

3. Coordinate with the Relevant Hospital for Evaluation

We liaise with the international medical department at one or more partner hospitals so attending specialists in the relevant discipline can evaluate your case file and address preliminary questions.

Patient Action Prepares questions for the specialist and participates in a scheduled remote video consultation if recommended.
Facilitator Coordination Role Schedules cross-timezone tele-consultations and arranges a qualified medical interpreter if needed.
Hospital Clinical Role Licensed specialists evaluate the medical records and conduct the clinical tele-consultation.
Step 04

4. Receive Hospital-Provided Treatment Information & Proposed Arrangements

The hospital issues a written preliminary medical response outlining proposed diagnostic or treatment steps, estimated inpatient/outpatient duration, and an itemized provisional cost estimate from the hospital billing office.

Patient Action Reviews the hospital-provided proposal at their own pace, consulting their local physician at home as desired.
Facilitator Coordination Role Helps clarify administrative items, hospital stay logistics, and estimated post-discharge local stay timelines.
Hospital Clinical Role Issues the official clinical evaluation summary, provisional treatment outline, and hospital fee estimate.
Step 05

5. Coordinate Travel, Visa Guidance, Accommodation & Transfers

Once you confirm your appointment dates with the hospital, our travel coordination desk assists with medical visa documentation, accessible hotel or recovery residence bookings, and airport meet-and-greet transfers.

Patient Action Submits passport details for visa invitation letters and selects preferred accommodation options for themselves and companions.
Facilitator Coordination Role Coordinates visa guidance, recovery-ready accommodation near the hospital, and airport/clinic ground transfers.
Hospital Clinical Role Issues official medical appointment confirmation and visa sponsorship letters for the relevant embassy.
Step 06

6. Arrive for Treatment & Receive Agreed Patient Coordination Support

Upon arrival in the destination city, you are greeted and transferred to your accommodation or hospital. During your hospital visits, international desk liaisons and interpreters support your communication needs.

Patient Action Attends in-person pre-procedure diagnostic examinations and clinical consultations at the hospital.
Facilitator Coordination Role Provides agreed on-site or on-call logistical support, interpreter coordination, and family companion assistance.
Hospital Clinical Role Conducts in-person examinations, confirms final clinical suitability, obtains informed consent, and delivers medical care.
Step 07

7. Coordinate Follow-Up Communication with the Treating Provider

Before you return home, the treating physician confirms fitness-to-fly clearance and provides comprehensive discharge records. After your return, we help coordinate remote follow-up communication with the hospital team.

Patient Action Shares translated hospital discharge summaries with their local primary physician and attends remote check-ins.
Facilitator Coordination Role Assists with obtaining translated medical reports, imaging copies, and scheduling post-return tele-follow-ups.
Hospital Clinical Role Issues discharge summaries, medication instructions, fit-to-fly clearance, and conducts clinical follow-up reviews.
Ethical & Clinical Transparency

Distinguishing Our Facilitation Role from the Hospital's Medical Role

For your safety and confidence, it is essential to understand how responsibilities are divided between Aurelia Global Health and your treating hospital.

Aurelia Global Health

Our Non-Clinical Coordination Role

We act strictly as an independent medical travel facilitator and patient support service:

  • Providing general, educational information about international hospital networks and destinations.
  • Routing your enquiry and medical records securely to hospital international desks.
  • Scheduling remote tele-consultations and coordinating certified medical interpreters.
  • Assisting with medical visa documentation, flights, accessible hotels, and ground transfers.
  • Facilitating post-discharge document collection and follow-up communication.
Partner Healthcare Provider

The Hospital's Clinical Decision-Making Role

All medical judgments and healthcare services rest solely with the licensed hospital and its clinicians:

  • Reviewing diagnostic records and determining whether a patient is a candidate for evaluation.
  • Conducting clinical examinations, diagnostics, and medical or surgical procedures.
  • Discussing clinical risks, alternatives, expected recovery, and obtaining informed consent.
  • Issuing official treatment plans, hospital cost estimates, and medical prescriptions.
  • Determining inpatient discharge readiness and medical fitness for return air travel.
Travel & On-Ground Logistics

International Travel & Companion Assistance

Coordinated travel arrangements designed around post-procedure comfort, accessibility, and flexible scheduling.

Medical Visa Guidance

Assistance obtaining official hospital appointment letters and understanding embassy requirements for patients and companions.

Accessible Stays

Curated hotel and serviced apartment options with step-free access, elevators, and proximity to the hospital campus.

Airport & Clinic Transfers

Pre-arranged arrival pickup at the terminal and scheduled transfers for outpatient appointments and follow-up visits.

Flexible Flight Planning

Guidance on selecting changeable airfares and requesting airline mobility or cabin assistance for your return journey.
