Why Choose Aurelia Global Health

Structured Coordination Built Around Patient Peace of Mind

Seeking medical care in another country involves logistical, linguistic, and administrative complexity. We serve as your dedicated coordination bridge before, during, and after your hospital visit.

01

Clear Separation of Facilitation & Clinical Care

We never make clinical diagnoses or push specific procedures. All medical evaluations and treatment plans come directly from licensed physicians at the treating hospital.

02

Transparent Hospital Enquiry Process

Receive written hospital-issued preliminary assessments, itemized cost estimates directly from the hospital's international billing desk, and clear timelines before booking travel.

03

Compassionate Companion & Travel Support

From medical visa invitation letters and accessible hotel selection to airport reception and medical interpreters, we support both patients and accompanying family members.

Patient Privacy First

Secure Medical Document Handling Protocol

We never collect sensitive diagnostic files through public web forms. After your initial enquiry, our team arranges a dedicated, encrypted hospital transfer link for medical records.

How It Works

A Simple, Reassuring 4-Step Patient Journey

From your initial enquiry to post-return follow-up communication, every stage is structured so you remain informed and supported.

01

Submit Enquiry & Share Records

Share your treatment category of interest and preferred destination. We provide a secure channel to forward existing medical reports to the hospital.

02

Hospital Review & Care Information

Specialists at the partner hospital review your file, issue preliminary clinical information, and arrange a remote video consultation if appropriate.

03

Travel, Visa & Stay Coordination

Once you decide to proceed with the hospital, we help coordinate medical visa documentation, recovery-appropriate accommodation, and airport transfers.

04

On-Site Support & Continuity of Care

Receive agreed arrival coordination, in-hospital interpretation, discharge documentation in English, and follow-up communication support after returning home.

Comprehensive Facilitation

International Patient Coordination Services

Practical, non-clinical assistance tailored to the needs of international patients and their accompanying family members.

Hospital Case Liaison

Structured submission of medical summaries and diagnostic scans to international hospital desks to obtain timely specialist feedback.

Pre-Travel Tele-Consultations

Scheduling video introductions between you, the attending hospital specialist, and a medical interpreter across global timezones.

Medical Visa Documentation

Coordination of official hospital appointment confirmation letters and visa sponsorship documents required by embassies.

Recovery-Ready Accommodation

Assistance booking accessible hotels or serviced recovery apartments located in close proximity to the treating medical campus.

Medical Interpretation Support

Arrangement of qualified in-person or remote medical interpreters for consultations, consent briefings, and discharge reviews.

Airport & Clinic Transfers

Coordinated arrival greeting and comfortable ground transport between the international airport, hotel, and hospital departments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Medical Travel Facilitation

Clear answers regarding our coordination role, hospital evaluations, privacy protocols, and travel logistics.

Aurelia Global Health is an independent medical travel facilitator, not a healthcare provider. We coordinate non-clinical aspects of your journey—including hospital enquiry routing, tele-consultation scheduling, medical visa documentation guidance, accommodation, and ground transfers. All medical evaluations, clinical recommendations, and treatments are performed exclusively by licensed healthcare professionals at the treating hospital.
We do not ask you to upload sensitive medical records through general website contact forms. Once you submit a preliminary enquiry, your coordinator provides a dedicated, encrypted hospital transfer channel so your diagnostic reports and imaging can be reviewed directly by the hospital's international medical team.
Every preliminary treatment outline and financial estimate is prepared and issued by the partner hospital following their review of your medical documentation. Final clinical suitability and treatment plans are always confirmed in person by the attending physician upon arrival and examination.
