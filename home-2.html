Visual Patient Journey Timeline

How Your Cross-Border Care Is Coordinated

A structured milestone timeline showing how Aurelia Global Health coordinates with both you and your chosen hospital.

Milestone 1 • Days 1–2

Enquiry & Encrypted Record Transfer

You submit a general enquiry. We assign a dedicated coordinator and provide a secure transfer channel for your diagnostic reports.

Milestone 2 • Days 3–7

Hospital Review & Tele-Consultation

The hospital's clinical team reviews your records, provides preliminary treatment information, and meets with you via video call.

Milestone 3 • Pre-Departure

Visa, Flights & Recovery Stay

We assist with official hospital visa invitation letters, accessible accommodation near the facility, and airport reception scheduling.

Milestone 4 • Arrival & Return

Admission Support & Follow-Up

In-person hospital desk coordination, medical interpretation, translated discharge records, and post-return communication facilitation.
