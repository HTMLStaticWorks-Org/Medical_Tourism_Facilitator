Thoughtful International Healthcare Coordination & Patient Advocacy

Aurelia Global Health is designed around a singular principle: patients exploring healthcare abroad deserve clear communication, honest boundaries between logistics and medicine, and attentive support at every step.

Company Overview

Who We Are & How We Serve International Patients

Navigating healthcare in a foreign country requires far more than booking a flight. Language barriers, unfamiliar hospital administrative processes, medical record formatting, and companion logistics can quickly overwhelm patients and families.

Aurelia Global Health acts as a structured, non-clinical bridge between international patients and hospital international desks. We help organize your enquiry, facilitate secure record transmission to the hospital's evaluating specialists, coordinate remote video consultations, and manage practical travel arrangements.

By removing administrative friction while respecting the independent clinical authority of treating physicians, we empower patients to make informed, unhurried decisions in consultation with their families and home doctors.

Mission, Values & Ethics

Our Core Pillars of Facilitation

Every interaction with our coordination desk is guided by strict transparency and patient-first ethics.

01

Commitment to Transparency

We clearly distinguish non-clinical facilitation from medical practice. All clinical evaluations, treatment plans, and hospital fee estimates originate directly from the treating hospital—never fabricated or marked up with hidden clinical claims.

02

Patient Support Philosophy

Every patient's cultural background, mobility needs, and family structure are unique. Our coordinators tailor travel timing, interpretation support, and accommodation proximity to each individual's comfort.

03

Confidentiality & Data Care

We never request diagnostic medical uploads through ordinary public contact forms. Sensitive clinical records are routed only through encrypted channels after coordinator verification.

Coordination Methodology

Our International Patient Coordination Approach

We organize our work into three distinct phases so nothing is overlooked before departure, during hospital admission, or after returning home.

Phase I: Pre-Travel Clarity

Consolidating medical histories, arranging certified translations where needed, routing files to hospital departments, and scheduling preliminary specialist video calls.

Phase II: On-Ground Hospitality & Liaison

Coordinating visa letters, airport reception, accessible recovery stays, companion logistics, and in-hospital medical interpretation.

Phase III: Post-Discharge Continuity

Ensuring patients receive complete translated discharge documentation for their home doctor and coordinating remote follow-up check-ins with the treating specialist.

Ethical Boundaries

What We Do — And What We Never Do

What We Coordinate:

  • Hospital enquiry routing & appointment scheduling
  • Medical interpreter & tele-consultation logistics
  • Medical visa letters, accommodation & airport transfers

What We Never Do:

  • We never practice medicine or diagnose medical conditions
  • We never promise clinical outcomes or guaranteed recovery
  • We never override the clinical judgment of licensed doctors
