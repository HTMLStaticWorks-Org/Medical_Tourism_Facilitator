How Clinical Evaluation Works

What Happens After You Select a Treatment Category?

Selecting a category on our website initiates a non-clinical coordination enquiry—never an automated medical booking.

  • 1. Coordinator Briefing: We discuss your preferred destination countries, timeline, and language preferences.
  • 2. Secure Record Transfer: We provide an encrypted hospital link so you can share recent diagnostic reports or imaging directly with the hospital desk.
  • 3. Specialist Review: The hospital's clinical department reviews your documentation and advises whether an in-person evaluation or tele-consultation is appropriate.
